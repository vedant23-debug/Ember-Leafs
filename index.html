Brewing guides

Brewing green tea

Use water around 75–80 °C and steep for about two minutes. Hotter water brings out bitterness.

Brewing black tea

Fully boiling water, three to four minutes. Taste at three and adjust.

Pour-over coffee

About 1:16 coffee to water, medium-fine grind, finish in under four minutes.
